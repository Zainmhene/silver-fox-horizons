01Our mandate

At the intersection of three disciplines.

Strong states, credible voices and patient capital rise together.

Africa’s governments are reforming, its nations are asserting their place in the world, and international investors are looking harder at the continent than ever before. These ambitions are connected, yet advice is usually offered in separate silos.

We work at the intersection of statecraft, international positioning and investment diplomacy. That lets us help a ministry improve delivery, help a nation tell the story of that progress, and help investors and governments build partnerships on firmer ground.

Our clients are governments and heads of state’s offices, ministries and public institutions, development institutions, and international companies and investors.

02How we work

An approach that looks further.

  1. 01

    Listen first

    Every engagement begins with the people who will own the result: their priorities, constraints and context.

  2. 02

    Diagnose with candour

    Clear, evidence-led assessments, including the uncomfortable ones. Good advice is honest advice.

  3. 03

    Design for ownership

    Plans are built with, not for, the institutions that will deliver them, so they survive our departure.

  4. 04

    Deliver alongside

    We stay close to implementation and adjust as reality tests the plan.

  5. 05

    Tell the story well

    Progress that is not communicated is rarely credited. We help partners own their narrative.

03Principles

What you can expect of us.

i.

Discretion as standard

Much of our work is sensitive. We treat confidentiality as a professional obligation, not a feature.

ii.

African context, global standard

We bring international best practice and adapt it to the realities of each country, never the reverse.

iii.

The long view

We measure success by what endures after an engagement ends: capability, credibility and trust.
