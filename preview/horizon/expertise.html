Chapter 01

Statecraft, Governance & Institutional Transformation

What we do

Reform is rarely short of ideas. It is short of capacity, sequencing and follow-through. We work alongside leaders and senior officials to turn priorities into delivery.

Helping governments strengthen institutional capacity, design and implement reforms, and improve public-sector performance. From the centre of government to individual ministries, we help design reforms that fit the institutions that must carry them, and build the routines that keep them on track.

Who it’s for

  • Heads of state’s and government offices
  • Ministries and public agencies
  • Development institutions supporting reform

Typical engagements

  • Design and set-up of delivery and implementation units
  • Reform strategy, prioritisation and sequencing
  • Institutional and organisational reviews
  • Performance management and results frameworks
  • Advisory support to heads of government and ministers
  • Leadership and capability programmes for senior officials

Chapter 02

Nation Branding, Strategic Communications & Global Positioning

What we do

A nation’s reputation shapes who visits, who invests and who listens. We help governments be understood on their own terms.

Supporting governments in strengthening their international reputation, soft power and global visibility. We combine strategy, narrative and disciplined communications so that a country’s real progress is visible to the audiences that matter, at home and abroad.

Who it’s for

  • Presidencies and ministries of foreign affairs
  • Investment promotion and tourism agencies
  • Public institutions with an international audience

Typical engagements

  • National brand strategy and narrative development
  • Strategic communications for reform programmes and major moments
  • International media and stakeholder engagement
  • Soft power and cultural diplomacy strategies
  • Reputation risk and crisis communications
  • Positioning for summits, bids and global forums

Chapter 03

Investment Diplomacy, Market Access & Strategic Partnerships

What we do

Capital moves towards clarity. We help investors and governments find each other, understand each other and build partnerships that last.

Helping international companies and investors navigate African markets, engage with governments and develop strategic investment and commercial partnerships. For companies and investors, we provide the context and the conversations needed to enter and grow in African markets. For governments, we help shape propositions that serious investors can act on.

Who it’s for

  • International companies and investors
  • Development finance institutions
  • Investment promotion agencies
  • Ministries of trade, finance and industry

Typical engagements

  • Market entry strategy and political-economy context
  • Government engagement and stakeholder mapping
  • Preparation for first meetings with government
  • Partnership identification and structuring support
  • Investment promotion strategy for governments
  • Support for public–private partnership dialogue

Which chapter is yours?

Tell us about the reform, the reputation or the partnership you are working on. We will tell you honestly whether and how we can help.
