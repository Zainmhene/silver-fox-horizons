About

An advisory withan institution’s taste.

Silver Fox Horizons partners with governments, institutions and businesses to strengthen state capacity, elevate global influence and unlock investment opportunities across Africa.

Our mandate

At the intersection of statecraft, positioning and investment.

Africa’s next chapter will be written by institutions that deliver, by nations that are understood on their own terms, and by partnerships that create lasting value on both sides.

We bring these threads together. Our work spans the design and delivery of public-sector reform, the strategic communication of national ambition, and the patient diplomacy that brings governments and investors to the same table.

We serve governments and offices of heads of state, ministries and public institutions, development institutions, and the international companies and investors who want to build in Africa responsibly.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to.

Six commitments that shape how we advise, whom we work with and how we measure success.

01

Discretion

We work quietly and in confidence. The credit belongs to the institutions we serve.

02

Rigour

Evidence before opinion. Every recommendation is tested against the political and economic reality.

03

Delivery over rhetoric

Strategy matters only when it changes outcomes. We design for implementation from the start.

04

Local insight, global reach

Grounded in African contexts, fluent in the expectations of international partners.

05

Long-term partnership

We measure our work by what endures after the engagement ends.

06

Independence

Candid advice, free of conflicts, in service of the mandate and nothing else.

How we work

Quiet rigour, from first
conversation to delivery.

Every engagement is shaped around the mandate, the institutions involved and the political reality on the ground. Four movements guide the work.

01

Listen

We begin with the mandate behind the brief: the context, the stakeholders and what success must look like.

02

Diagnose & design

Rigorous analysis becomes a clear strategy, sequenced for delivery and alert to political economy.

03

Convene

We bring the right counterparts to the table, across government, capital and international partners.

04

Deliver & embed

We stay through implementation and build capability, so results outlast the engagement.
