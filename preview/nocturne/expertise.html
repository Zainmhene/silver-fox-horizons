01

Statecraft, Governance & Institutional Transformation

Strong institutions are the foundation of every national ambition. We work alongside leaders to turn priorities into reforms that are designed well, sequenced wisely and delivered.

What we do

We help governments strengthen institutional capacity, design and implement reforms, and improve public-sector performance, from the centre of government to the front line of service delivery.

Typical engagements

  • Reform strategy, prioritisation and sequencing
  • Design and set-up of delivery units
  • Institutional and organisational diagnostics
  • Performance management and public-sector productivity
  • Cabinet, centre-of-government and leadership support

Who it’s for

  • Offices of heads of state and government
  • Ministries and public institutions
  • Development institutions supporting reform
02

Nation Branding, Strategic Communications & Global Positioning

How a nation is seen shapes what it can achieve: in trade, in tourism, in diplomacy and in investment. We help governments earn and project the reputation they deserve.

What we do

We support governments in strengthening their international reputation, soft power and global visibility, grounding every narrative in substance and delivery.

Typical engagements

  • National narrative and nation brand strategy
  • Strategic communications for reform programmes
  • Positioning for summits, forums and state visits
  • International media and stakeholder engagement
  • Soft power and cultural diplomacy programmes

Who it’s for

  • Presidencies and communications offices
  • Ministries of foreign affairs, trade and tourism
  • Investment promotion and national agencies
03

Investment Diplomacy, Market Access & Strategic Partnerships

Capital needs counterparts it can trust. We help investors and governments find one another, understand one another and build partnerships designed to last.

What we do

We help international companies and investors navigate African markets, engage with governments and develop strategic investment and commercial partnerships.

Typical engagements

  • Market entry strategy and political-economy analysis
  • Structured engagement with government counterparts
  • Strategic partnership design and structuring
  • Investment missions, roadshows and delegations
  • Stakeholder mapping and relationship management

Who it’s for

  • International companies and investors
  • Sovereign and institutional capital
  • Governments seeking strategic partners
